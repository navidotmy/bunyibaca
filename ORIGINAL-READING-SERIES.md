# Original BunyiBaca graded reading series (v2)

This independently created educational resource uses *general* literacy instruction techniques: phonemic awareness, systematic grapheme–phoneme correspondences, continuous blending, controlled vocabulary, simple sentences, shared stories and formative checks. The eight-stage format is a general curriculum design choice, not a page-for-page adaptation of another publisher's books.

BunyiBaca is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed from Bacalah Anakku, ReadNetwork, or the original authors. Do not reuse the original books’ prose, page designs, stories, artwork, audio or branded exercises.

## The eight original books

1. Bunyi Mula — hearing and blending the first vowel/consonant sounds.
2. Suku Kata Bergerak — KV syllables with additional common consonants.
3. Jelajah Huruf — new graphemes and meaningful KVKV words.
4. Kata Jadi Cerita — vowel o and conversational phrases.
5. Hujung Perkataan — closed syllables and final consonants.
6. Dua Huruf Satu Bunyi — digraphs ng, ny, sy and kh.
7. Vokal Berwarna — e-pepet/e-taling, diphthongs and hiatus.
8. Pembaca Berdikari — morphology, longer reading and comprehension.

Every book includes eight original pages: sound discovery, blending, sound ladder, graded word list, word comparison, sentence work, an original parent-supported reading passage and a check. The first three books (24 pages) are free in addition to the existing 32 free lessons. Later books show only a first-page preview until a secure entitlement service is implemented.

**Important:** Reading an entire page marks it **practised**, not mastered. The child may need adult assistance with words not yet taught; passages are explicitly labelled as shared reading. The application makes no validated efficacy claim. No payment gateway is active.

**Audio caution:** The bundled audio is unreviewed synthetic output. Human-recorded or linguistically validated speech assets are strongly recommended, especially for isolated consonants, Malay digraphs and the two e sounds. Never present these generated clips as certified native pronunciation.
